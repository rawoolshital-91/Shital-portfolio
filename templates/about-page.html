ABOUT ME

Shital Rawool

I'm Shital Balkrishna Rawool,

I began my career in 2016 with Tata Communications as an Associate Network Engineer, where I worked for nearly four years. I initially joined as a third-party employee and was later selected for a payroll position as a Customer Service Executive. During my tenure, I was part of the GPD team, responsible for managing the top 50 domestic and international customers of Tata Communications. Subsequently, I joined GTT Communications as a Network Analyst. However, due to a mismatch between the promised role and the actual responsibilities, I decided to move on after seven months. I then joined Vodafone as an Assistant Manager, where I worked in a completely different testing environment. In this role, I collaborated closely with stakeholders and vendors while working on networking products. Over time, I decided to transition my career path. I realized that the networking field primarily involved shift-based roles, regardless of seniority, which motivated me to explore my interest in programming. I pursued a Full Stack Python Developer course. completed few projects, and successfully transitioned into the IT domain. This led to an opportunity with Birla soft as a Consultant Specialist, where I worked for their client Northern Trust.

My core expertise lies in Python, with extensive experience in Django and Flask for developing scalable and efficient web applications. I am passionate about building solutions that integrate robust backend functionality with clean, responsive user interfaces, delivering seamless and engaging digital experiences..
